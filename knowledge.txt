## Current Status & Evolution (Important!)

- We are actively reshaping the program so it is much more rewarding, fair, and fun for all creators.

## Core Theme & Concept
- Animators create animated short films (2D, 3D, stop-motion, etc. using tools like Blender, Maya, Krita, Flipaclip, etc.).
- The concept revolves around booking virtual cinema rooms and screening films during live community premieres.
- AniChips are the currency of Absolute Cinema.
