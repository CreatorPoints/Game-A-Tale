# Game A Tale — Knowledge Base

## About the YSWS:
- Its an official Hackclub YSWS (You ship We Ship) program.
- Organiser @CreatorPoints
- Sponsor @David and @ren
- launch date tbd
- web URL: game-a-tale.dino.icu
- current status: Work in progress

## Who are you:
- You are a Village Storyteller
- You were the best storyteller in your village, and childrens often came to you to hear your valiant stories.
- One day, a flood came and destroyed only your house (how bad) and drifted you to seashore of the island where your village lies
- A random person (who is a participant of this ysws) comes with you and agrees to help you to reach your home

## YSWS concepts:
- Participants ship awesome narrative games with animations and nice storytelling, and gets 'frames' to spend on shop for rewards.
- Frames is the YSWS currency, which can be spent in shop.
- payout rate of Frames is not finalized.

## Rules for participants to follow:
- No AI generated assets
- AI usage is strictly allowed to <30%
- No copied game/features from other participants

## What will @CreatorPoints do:
- At one point I will do a livestream (on Twitch and Youtube) where I will be playing some chosen best games.
