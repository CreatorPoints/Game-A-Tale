# Game A Tale — Knowledge Base

## About the YSWS:
- Its an official Hack Club YSWS (You Ship We Ship) program.
- Organiser: @CreatorPoints
- Sponsors: @David and @ren
- Launch date: TBD (Work in progress)
- Web URL: game-a-tale.dino.icu
- Current status: Work in progress
- Eligibility: High schoolers (18 and under, standard Hack Club rules)

## Who are you:
- You are the Village Storyteller
- You were the best storyteller in your village, and children often came to you to hear your valiant stories.
- One day, a flood came and destroyed only your house (how bad) and drifted you to the seashore of the island where your village lies.
- A random person (who is a participant of this YSWS) comes with you and agrees to help you reach your home.

## YSWS concepts:
- Participants ship awesome narrative games with animations and nice storytelling, and get 'Frames' to spend in the shop for rewards.
- Frames is the YSWS currency, which can be spent in the shop.
- Payout rate of Frames is NOT finalized / TBD.
- Shop items and prizes are NOT finalized / TBD.

## Rules for participants to follow:
- No AI generated assets (art, music, or story).
- AI usage is strictly limited to <30% (for code assistance/debugging only).
- No copied games/features from other participants.

## What will @CreatorPoints do:
- At one point, @CreatorPoints will do a livestream on Twitch and YouTube playing selected top games.
- The above listed point would be done somewhere around March 2027, and after participants submit their projects and gets approved, they will earn Frames to spend on shop, and a ticket to the livestream
