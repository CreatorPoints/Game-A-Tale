What's This "YSWS"?

YSWS is a "You Ship We Ship" program where you make a fire game, with rich storytelling and creativity. Oooh... @CreatorPoints says that he would even play some of the best games made, handpicked!

What's there in the village?

When you reach the village, your story is told all over the village, and villagers reward your valiant stories with rewards like

  • $100 Steam Developer Grant — To list your awesome game on Steam!
  • $5 Steam Gift Card — An excuse to play games lol!
  • Drawing Tab Grant — Draw better characters :3

And much more!

Whats Hack Club?

Hack Club is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organisation that empowers teenagers aged 13–18 to learn programming, engineering, and technology through hands-on projects, hackathons, and community-driven initiatives. It provides resources, mentorship, and opportunities for young creators to turn their ideas into real-world projects.
