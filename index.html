Game A Tale

A Hack Club YSWS where you ship a narrative game with an Oscar-worthy story.

What Is It?

A game jam dedicated to storytelling. Focus on rich characters, compelling worldbuilding, and interactive narrative design.

The Goal

Build and ship your narrative game. Selected top games will be featured in a live playthrough stream on YouTube and Twitch with @CreatorPoints.

FAQ

Open to high schoolers worldwide. Build in any engine you like, ship an open source repository, and share your playable build.
